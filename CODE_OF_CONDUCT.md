# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive. Welcome people of every background and experience level. Criticise ideas and code, not people. Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable, here or in any space connected to the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub ([@Mohammad-AlBaker-Zaytoun](https://github.com/Mohammad-AlBaker-Zaytoun)). Reports are handled confidentially.
